# 👑 Princess Day - Magical Dark Luxury Website For Her ✨

A romantic, royal starry midnight website created to celebrate your girl on **Princess Day**, featuring:
- **All 4 Chapters in a stunning, luxury dark starry aesthetic**
- **Dynamic romantic background transitions (midnight rose, enchanted twilight, passionate crimson, celestial gold)**
- **Song Player with a romantic welcome popup** asking her to play your song
- **Built-in romantic music-box melody backup** if no custom MP3 is added yet
- **Gilded photo frames** for her 4 pictures
- **Interactive crowning celebration with confetti on Page 4**
- **100% Mobile & Desktop responsive**

---

## 🎵 How The Song Feature Works

When she opens the website, a romantic royal popup greets her:
> *"👑 A Melody for My Princess 🎵  
> Would you like to play our special song while you explore your royal chapters?"*

When she clicks **"✨ Yes, Play Our Song"**:
- It automatically plays your song!
- A golden notification pill appears: *"Now Playing: Our Special Song 💖"*
- She can pause or resume music anytime using the music button in the top bar.

### Adding Your Own Song:
1. Choose an MP3 audio file of your special song (e.g. your couple's song).
2. Place it in this project folder and rename it:
   **`song.mp3`**
3. That's it! When you push to GitHub, the website will play your song directly!
4. *(Bonus: If you don't add an MP3 file, the site automatically plays a sweet gentle synthesized music-box lullaby so there is never silence!)*

## 🖼️ Adding Her 4 Photos

Copy her 4 photos into the `images/` folder named:
- `photo1.jpg` (Chapter 1)
- `photo2.jpg` (Chapter 2)
- `photo3.jpg` (Chapter 3)

## 🚀 How to Host on GitHub Pages (Free)

1. Go to [GitHub.com](https://github.com/new) and create a **Public** repository (e.g., `for-my-princess`).
2. Drag and drop all the files from this folder (`index.html`, `style.css`, `script.js`, `config.js`, `song.mp3`, and the `images/` folder) into GitHub.
3. Go to **Settings** → **Pages** (on the left menu).
4. Under **Branch**, select **main** and click **Save**.
5. In ~1 minute, GitHub will give you a live link to send to her:
   `https://YOUR_USERNAME.github.io/for-my-princess/`
